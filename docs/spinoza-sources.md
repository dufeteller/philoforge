All explanatory summaries are paraphrases. Source excerpts in the English interface are drawn from Elwes; French passages are our translations. Lean's `depends` relation is a conceptual abstraction, not a full theory of knowledge. Cause and production are not asserted to be synonymous in all contexts. No proof of God's existence, substance monism, or the full Ethics is included.

The optional counterexample is not Spinoza's statement: it hypothesizes a causal pair with no commonality, which conflicts with A4 + A5. The contradiction proof refutes that added hypothesis under the reconstruction, not the consistency of Spinoza's axioms with one another.
